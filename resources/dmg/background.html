Install Clod
Drag Clod onto the Applications folder

Opening Clod for the first time

  1. Open Clod from Applications. macOS says it can't check it. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Next to "Clod was blocked", click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
You only do this once. Clod then walks you through the rest of setup.
